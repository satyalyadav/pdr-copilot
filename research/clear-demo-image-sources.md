# Clear dent repair image sources

Research on 2026-11-04. I downloaded and visually inspected the images linked below, each under 5 MB. These are first-party shop or manufacturer presentations, not independently verified repair records. Reuse licenses are unknown; obtain permission before distributing the images. The copies in `test-inputs/real-photos/` are local-only and ignored by Git for that reason. The original inspection files were in `/tmp/pdr-source-inspect/`.

## Strongest clear before/after pairs

1. **Dent Time, Rivian R1S rear quarter.** [Shop page](https://denttime.com/); direct images: [before](https://denttime.com/media/repairs/rivian-before.webp), [after](https://denttime.com/media/repairs/rivian-after.webp). The before image shows a very obvious deep depression below the tail light; the after shows the corresponding area without the large depression. Tail light, seam, bumper and panel shape support the shop's same-case pairing. The framing shifts slightly, and reflections and exposure change. No striped reflection board is visible. The site claims a real repair; these are two endpoint images, not documented intermediate stages. Inspected `rivian-before.webp` and `rivian-after.webp`.

2. **Dent Time, large silver door dent.** [Shop page](https://denttime.com/); direct images: [before](https://denttime.com/media/tiers-bigdent/large-dent-before-paintless-repair.webp), [practical result](https://denttime.com/media/tiers-bigdent/practical.webp), [premium result](https://denttime.com/media/tiers-bigdent/premium.webp). The before image has an unmistakable deep crease across the body line. Both result images show the same-looking door and handle with the large crease absent, under changed reflections and slightly different framing. The shop presents both results against the same before image, but its page does not establish that practical and premium are sequential stages of one repair; do not present them as a verified timeline. No useful full-height stripe pattern is visible. Inspected `big-before.webp`, `big-practical.webp` and `big-premium.webp`.

3. **KECO, 2018 Dacia Duster crease.** [Manufacturer case page](https://www.kecotabs.com/blogs/all/2018-dacia-duster-gpr-before-after); [direct 640-pixel composite](https://www.kecotabs.com/cdn/shop/articles/daciaduster.jpg?v=1594220998&width=640); [embedded video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W6wjnPnN4lY). The side-by-side image shows a conspicuous pinched and bent light reflection at left and a smoother reflection at right. The door seam and red markings support the same-case pairing claimed by KECO. Viewpoint and reflection shape differ, and the composite splits the image into two narrow panels. I inspected the still, not the video, so the video may contain useful intermediate work but its stages are unverified. Inspected `dacia2018.jpg`.

## Reflection-light teaching example

**Dent Time, red fender.** [Shop page](https://denttime.com/door-ding-repair/); direct images: [before](https://denttime.com/media/door-ding-before.webp), [after](https://denttime.com/media/door-ding-after.jpg). The before image has a clearly visible pinched stripe, with a circular magnified inset; the after shows a smoother stripe at the corresponding location. The source explicitly calls it the same panel and reflection board. The framing, exposure and reflection position vary, and the stripe board and inset are present in the images. The visible dent is smaller than the Rivian or silver-door damage. These are claimed before/after endpoints, not independently established intermediate stages. Inspected `lines-before.webp` and `lines-after.jpg`.

## Gaps and compatibility

None of these examples has a verified sequence of intermediate repair stages. The video was not inspected, and no force or synchronized measurement data was verified. The app requires at least three near-vertical bright stripe components extending at least 65% of image height, with reference fitting outside a manually selected area. The parent subsequently copied the Rivian, silver-door and red-fender pairs into [manual test inputs](../test-inputs/real-photos/README.md) and ran the current detector with its default selection. All six returned insufficient evidence. The red-fender after image produced some markers from one stripe, below the minimum evidence threshold. The saved JSON records exact results. These are useful images for model review and visual explanation, not a demonstrated OpenCV success.
